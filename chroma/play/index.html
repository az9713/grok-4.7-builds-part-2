What the bench uses

  1. X, Y, and Z are the CIE 1931 2° colour-matching values at that wavelength.
  2. Linear sRGB uses the IEC 61966-2-1 matrix. The first row is 3.2404542, −1.5371385, −0.4985314.
  3. The second row is −0.9692660, 1.8760108, 0.0415560. The third row is 0.0556434, −0.2040259, 1.0572252.
  4. Each linear channel is clipped to 0..1. Then the sRGB curve is applied. At or below 0.0031308 the curve is 12.92 times the channel. Above that it is 1.055 times the channel to the power 1/2.4, minus 0.055.
  5. 555 nm reads green after the clip. The bytes are 97, 255, 0. The line says 555 nm reads green after the clip.
  6. 650 nm reads red after the clip. The bytes are 225, 0, 0. The line says 650 nm reads red after the clip.
