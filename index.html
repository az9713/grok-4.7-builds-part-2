Round 1

01

Service Door

You walk one night-shift worker through one archive room. A guard walks the floor. A freight lift is on the right.

Do this. Press W, A, S, or D to walk. The arrow keys also walk. Walk to the guard. Press Space. The guard falls. Walk into the lift. The lift stays shut until the guard is down. Press R after the win to start again.
02

Service Door Reel

This is a film player for the same worker. You do not walk anyone.

Do this. Press Play. Press Prev or Next to change the shot. Press Split, Storyboard, or Footage to change the view. Press Space to start the film or to pause the film. The left and right arrow keys change the shot.
03

Locksmith

This page replays a saved art-production run. It does not run the script live.

Do this. Press Replay DAG. Each box is one step. A failed step is marked fail. A later step can still pass.
04

Phylum Tray

You place copies of one beetle into a glass box. Each copy is the same animal with a small change.

Do this. Click a picture in the tray. The animal falls into the box and walks. Click the glass to drop another copy at that point. Press Evacuate habitat to clear the box.
05

Sodium Night

This is a six-shot short film. You watch it. You do not move a character.

Do this. Press Play. Press Prev or Next to change the shot. Press Split, Storyboard, or Footage to change the view. The keys 1, 2, and 3 do the same three views. Press Space to start the film or to pause the film.
06

Closed Stack

This is a press page for the Service Door story. Read it like a studio handout.

Do this. Read the fact sheet and the synopsis. Press Play the slice to open the playable room.
07

Continuity Bench

This page scores pictures. The test is whether each picture keeps the same person, clothes, and light.

Do this. Read the green and red marks. Green means that check passed. Red means it failed. One red mark fails that picture. One failed picture fails the whole run. Click a picture to read the reasons.
08

Gimbal Goods

This is a shop page for one lamp. The pictures show the same lamp.

Do this. Click a picture to make it large. Press All plates, Studio, In situ, or Finish to filter. Press Add to hold. Press Hold. Press Stamp ticket. The hold remains after you reload the page.

Round 2

09

Twin Orbit

Two people must carry one battery to the socket on the right ledge.

Do this. The pilot uses W, A, S, and D. Press E to pick the battery up. Press F to throw it. The mechanic uses the arrow keys. Press the period key to pick up. Press the slash key to throw.
10

Yard Ledger

Two trains move on a yard. You do not drive the trains. You set the track switches.

Do this. Click a numbered switch. SW0 sends the train Ash 4 onto the side track. SW1 sends the train Brick 12 off the main track. A red cell means both trains claim the same track. Press Space to pause. Press Reset yard to start again.
11

Ash Court

This is a short card game with 18 cards. Your cards are face up. The other hand is face down until a card is played.

Do this. Click a card in your hand. The suits are Moth, Staff, and Cloak. If the other side played a suit first and you hold that suit, play a card of that suit. The highest card of that suit wins the group. Press Print rulebook for the full rules.
12

Glass Index

This is a museum page for one repaired glass vessel. The gold line marks the same object.

Do this. Press All plates, North window, Raking, or Night. The light changes. The vessel stays the same.
13

Dieline Press

One bottle holds three liquids. The bottle shape does not change.

Do this. Press Bergamot, Smoke, or Hibiscus. Press Print net to open the flat cut-out pattern of the package.
14

Night Shift OS

This is a small fake computer. The story is inside the mail program.

Do this. Click an icon in the left column. The icons are Mail, Map, and Kettle. One extra mail icon does nothing. A window opens. Drag the window by its title bar. Press × to close the window. Open Mail. The top bar changes from Shift open to Shift note read.
15

Kiln Reel

This is a short film of one bowl on a pottery wheel. You do not shape the clay.

Do this. Press 01 Throw, 02 Trim, or 03 Finish. Each button plays that stage of the same bowl.
16

Cutaway House

You see one house and a simple floor plan. A click changes the picture of that same house.

Do this. Click Kitchen on the plan. The picture changes to the kitchen. Click Front or Stair. The picture returns to the outside.
17

Broadcast Kit

This is a sports graphic for a fictional match. You change the score. You do not play the match.

Do this. Press Home +1 or Away +1. The score changes. Press Replay wipe for the transition. Press End card to show the end card. Press End card again to hide it.
18

Swarm Brief

This page shows the saved steps behind a short report on ship rust. It does not run the script live.

Do this. Read the boxes. A green border means that step passed. Open the explainer link for the written page.

Round 3

19

Apsis

One craft flies a circle around a planet. You do not steer left or right. You burn along its path.

Do this. Hold Prograde until the far point meets the green line. Wait until the craft reaches that far point. Hold Prograde again until the path is round. Press Reset plot to start again.
20

Spillway

Rain falls on a roof. Water walks downhill. The west floor drains into the archive. The east floor drains into the cistern.

Do this. Press Raise. On the row under the rain, raise each west tile twice. Press Open the sky. The cistern must hold the water. The archive must stay dry.
21

Sort Case

You set one line of metal type. The stick shows the line backwards. The proof prints it the right way.

Do this. Click sorts until the line is SOCKET DARK. Press Lock the chase. Press Pull a proof. The proof must match the ticket.
22

Four Partials

Four sine waves add into one tone. The amber line is their sum. The bench has no score.

Do this. Press Sound the partials. Move a ratio or a height. Drag the string to write a loudness curve. Press Silence to stop the sound.

Round 4

23

Snell

A ray climbs from liquid into air. Past the critical angle, the ray reflects.

Do this. Set the liquid index and the incidence angle. Put the exit ray on the bell. Index 1.33 at 19 degrees meets the bell.
24

Traverse

You chain from a peg to a mark. A pond blocks the straight east line. Bearings run clockwise from north.

Do this. Add legs of bearing and distance. North 3, then east 5, closes on the mark. A leg through the pond cannot win.
25

Heddle

You tie four shafts and throw eight picks. Risen warp covers the weft. The ticket is a 2/2 twill.

Do this. Tie treadle 1 to shafts 1 and 2, treadle 2 to 2 and 3, treadle 3 to 3 and 4, and treadle 4 to 4 and 1. Throw treadles 1, 2, 3, 4, then repeat.
26

Hour Angle

A gnomon casts a shadow from latitude, day, and solar hour. Declination uses Cooper's formula. The dial has no score.

Do this. Move the three sliders. At latitude 51.5, day 81, and hour 12, the altitude reads 38.5 degrees. A sun below the horizon leaves no shadow.

Cam

Do this. Choose 10 kΩ and 100 µF. That pair is the 1 second time constant. Any other pair in the bin misses the mark.
