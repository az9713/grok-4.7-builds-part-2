# Henry

The inductance is 1 H. Energy is current times current, divided by 2. Current is in amperes. Energy is in joules.

The bin is 2, 4, 6, 8, and 10 A. The mark is 18.0 J, within 0.2 J. Only 6 A hits. The other four read 2.0 J, 8.0 J, 32.0 J, and 50.0 J.
