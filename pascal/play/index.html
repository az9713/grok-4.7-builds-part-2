Rules

  1. Gauge pressure is (98 × depth) / 10 kilopascals. Depth is in metres.
  2. The mark is 49.0 kPa. A hit is within 0.2 kPa.
  3. 5 m is the hit. The line says The pressure meets the mark.
  4. 1 m reads 9.8 kPa. 2 m reads 19.6 kPa. 10 m reads 98.0 kPa. 20 m reads 196.0 kPa.
  5. Those four miss. The line says The pressure misses the mark.
