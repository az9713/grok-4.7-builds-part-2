Rules

  1. The current is fixed.
  2. Field is 40 / distance microtesla. Distance is in centimetres.
  3. The mark is 8.0 µT. A hit is within 0.2 µT.
  4. 5 cm is the hit. The line says The field meets the mark.
  5. 2 cm reads 20.0 µT. 4 cm reads 10.0 µT. 8 cm reads 5.0 µT. 10 cm reads 4.0 µT.
  6. Those four miss. The line says The field misses the mark.
