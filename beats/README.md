# Beats

One tone is fixed at 440 Hz. You choose the second tone. The beat is the absolute difference of the two tones.

The bin is 442, 444, 446, 448, and 450 Hz. The mark is 4 Hz, within 0.5 Hz. Only 444 Hz hits.

The curve is the envelope for 0.50 s.

Open `play/index.html`.
