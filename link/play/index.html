Rules

  1. The link count is posts × (posts - 1) / 2.
  2. The mark is 15 links. A hit is within 0.5 links.
  3. 6 posts is the hit. The line says The mesh meets the mark.
  4. 3 posts reads 3 links. 4 posts reads 6 links. 5 posts reads 10 links. 8 posts reads 28 links.
  5. Those four miss. The line says The mesh misses the mark.
