Rules

  1. Gravity is 9.81 m/s².
  2. Period is 2 × π × the square root of length / 9.81. Length is in metres. Period is in seconds.
  3. The mark is 2.00 s. A hit is within 0.02 s.
  4. 0.99 m is the hit. The line says The period meets the mark.
  5. 0.50 m reads 1.42 s. 0.75 m reads 1.74 s. 1.25 m reads 2.24 s. 1.60 m reads 2.54 s.
  6. Those four miss. The line says The period misses the mark.
