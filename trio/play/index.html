Trio · choose three

Set the total to 20 ways.

You choose how many items sit in the row. The total is the number of ways to choose three of them.

Rules

  1. The number of ways is items × (items - 1) × (items - 2) / 6.
  2. The mark is 20 ways. A hit is within 0.5 ways.
  3. 6 items is the hit. The line says The total meets the mark.
  4. 4 items reads 4 ways. 5 items reads 10 ways. 7 items reads 35 ways. 8 items reads 56 ways.
  5. Those four miss. The line says The total misses the mark.
