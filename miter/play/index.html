Rules

  1. The interior angle in degrees is 180 × (sides - 2) / sides.
  2. The mark is 120°. A hit is within 0.5°.
  3. 6 sides is the hit. The line says The angle meets the mark.
  4. 3 sides reads 60°. 4 sides reads 90°. 5 sides reads 108°. 8 sides reads 135°.
  5. Those four miss. The line says The angle misses the mark.
