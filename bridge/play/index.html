Rules

  1. The battery is 2 V. P is 100 Ω. Q is 200 Ω. R is 150 Ω.
  2. P and R form the left divider. Q and X form the right divider. R and X are the bottom arms.
  3. Galvanometer voltage is 2 × (R / (P + R) − X / (Q + X)).
  4. Null is X = 300 Ω. A hit is within 0.001 V of 0.
  5. 300 Ω says The galvanometer is at null. Every other value in the bin says The galvanometer is off null.
