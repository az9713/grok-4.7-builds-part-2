Four Partials · string bench

Add four sines.

A tone is a stack of sine waves. Partial 1 is the note you name. The other three are whole-number copies of that note. Their heights add. The amber line is that sum. This bench has no score.

Bow. Drag on the string. Height is loudness across a two-second loop.

Press Sound the partials. Then drag the string.

What the controls do

  1. Partial 1 starts at ratio 1. Partial 2 starts at 2. Partial 3 starts at 3. Partial 4 starts at 5.
  2. The minus and plus buttons change a ratio from 1 to 9. Ratio 2 is twice the fundamental. Ratio 3 is three times the fundamental.
  3. Each slider sets that partial’s height, from silent at 0 to full at 1.
  4. The fundamental slider sets the first partial from 110 Hz to 440 Hz. It starts at 220 Hz.
  5. The amber line shows one cycle of the fundamental. Each partial adds its sine at its own ratio.
  6. Drag on the string to write a loudness curve. The curve loops every 2 seconds while the bench sounds. Flat bow clears the curve to 0.70.
  7. Silence sets the loudness to 0. Sound the partials opens the audio device. A browser can refuse that device. The line then says “This browser did not open the audio device.” The drawing still works.
