Rules

  1. Angle 0 leaves the analyzer on the polarization axis. Intensity is 1.
  2. Intensity is cos² of the angle. Angle 90 gives intensity 0.
  3. The mark is 0.25. A hit is within 0.01.
  4. 60° is the hit. The line says The analyzer hits the mark.
  5. Any other whole angle says The beam misses the mark.
