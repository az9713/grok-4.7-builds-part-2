Rules

  1. Relative strain is force / 4. Force is in newtons. The readout has no unit.
  2. The mark is 3.00. A hit is within 0.02.
  3. 12 N is the hit. The line says The strain meets the mark.
  4. 4 N reads 1.00. 8 N reads 2.00. 16 N reads 4.00. 20 N reads 5.00.
  5. Those four miss. The line says The strain misses the mark.
