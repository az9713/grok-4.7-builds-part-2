Rules

  1. Frequency is 1000 / resistance hertz. Resistance is in kilohms.
  2. The mark is 200 Hz. A hit is within 1 Hz.
  3. 5 kΩ is the hit. The line says The frequency meets the mark.
  4. 2 kΩ reads 500 Hz. 4 kΩ reads 250 Hz. 8 kΩ reads 125 Hz. 10 kΩ reads 100 Hz.
  5. Those four miss. The line says The frequency misses the mark.
