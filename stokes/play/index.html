Rules

  1. Drag is 3 × radius newtons. Radius is in millimetres.
  2. The mark is 18 N. A hit is within 1 N.
  3. 6 mm is the hit. The line says The force meets the mark.
  4. 2 mm reads 6 N. 4 mm reads 12 N. 8 mm reads 24 N. 10 mm reads 30 N.
  5. Those four miss. The line says The force misses the mark.
