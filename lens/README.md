# Lens

A converging lens stands 30 cm from the object. Image distance v follows 1/v = 1/f − 1/30. The screen stands at 60 cm.

The bin is 10, 15, 20, 25, and 40 cm. A hit is within 2 cm of the screen. Only 20 cm lands on it. 40 cm gives no real image.
