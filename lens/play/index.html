Rules

  1. The object distance u is 30 cm. The lens is converging.
  2. Image distance follows 1/v = 1/f − 1/u.
  3. The screen stands at 60 cm. A hit is within 2 cm.
  4. 20 cm is the hit. The line says The image meets the screen.
  5. 10, 15, and 25 cm miss the screen. 40 cm forms no real image. Those lines say The image misses the screen. or The rays do not meet on the screen.
