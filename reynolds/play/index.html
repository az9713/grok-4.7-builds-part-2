Rules

  1. The Reynolds number is 50 × speed. Speed is in m/s.
  2. The mark is 400. A hit is within 1.
  3. 8 m/s is the hit. The line says The number meets the mark.
  4. 2 m/s reads 100. 4 m/s reads 200. 10 m/s reads 500. 16 m/s reads 800.
  5. Those four miss. The line says The number misses the mark.
