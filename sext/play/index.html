Sext · choose six

Set the sextet to 84.

You choose how many cards there are. The sextet is the number of ways to choose six of them.

Rules

  1. The sextet is cards × (cards − 1) × (cards − 2) × (cards − 3) × (cards − 4) × (cards − 5) / 720.
  2. The mark is 84. A hit is within 0.5.
  3. 9 cards is the hit. The line says The sextet meets the mark.
  4. 7 cards reads 7. 8 cards reads 28. 10 cards reads 210. 11 cards reads 462.
  5. Those four miss. The line says The sextet misses the mark.
