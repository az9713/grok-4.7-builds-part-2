Apsis · orbit plot

Raise the orbit. Then round it.

One craft flies a low circle around a planet. The green line is the outer orbit. You do not steer left or right. A burn only pushes along the craft’s path.

Coast. The far point must meet the green line. Then round the path.

Rules

  1. The craft starts on the inner circle. Fuel is 0.48.
  2. Hold Prograde. The burn pushes the craft forward. The far point rises. W and the up arrow do this too.
  3. Release the burn when the far point meets the green line. The green line sits at size 1.85.
  4. Wait. The craft coasts out to that far point.
  5. Hold Prograde again. Release when the path is nearly round. Eccentricity must fall under 0.08. Size must sit between 1.77 and 1.93.
  6. Hold that coast for 1.2 seconds. The line then says “Apsis closed. The orbit is round on the outer line.”
  7. Retrograde pushes backward. S and the down arrow do this. A long retrograde burn drops the craft onto the planet.
  8. A burn that uses all the fuel from the inner circle can leave the planet’s well.
  9. Press Reset plot, or press R, to start again.
