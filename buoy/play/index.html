Rules

  1. Buoyant force is 4 × volume newtons. Volume is in litres.
  2. The mark is 20 N. A hit is within 1 N.
  3. 5 L is the hit. The line says The force meets the mark.
  4. 1 L reads 4 N. 2 L reads 8 N. 8 L reads 32 N. 10 L reads 40 N.
  5. Those four miss. The line says The force misses the mark.
