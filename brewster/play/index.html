Rules

  1. The glass index is 1.50.
  2. The Brewster angle is atan(1.50). The page shows 56.3 degrees.
  3. The refracted angle is asin(sin(incidence) / 1.50).
  4. Incidence is a whole number from 40 to 70.
  5. A hit is within 0.5 degrees of 56.3.
  6. 56° is the only hit. The line says The rays meet at a right angle.
  7. 55° and 57° miss. Every other angle says The rays are not at a right angle.
