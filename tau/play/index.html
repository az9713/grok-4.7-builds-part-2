Rules

  1. The capacitance is fixed.
  2. Time is resistance / 5 seconds. Resistance is in kilohms.
  3. The mark is 3.00 s. A hit is within 0.02 s.
  4. 15 kΩ is the hit. The line says The time meets the mark.
  5. 5 kΩ reads 1.00 s. 10 kΩ reads 2.00 s. 20 kΩ reads 4.00 s. 25 kΩ reads 5.00 s.
  6. Those four miss. The line says The time misses the mark.
