Cam · plate and follower

Cut the rise, dwell, and fall.

A flat follower rides a plate cam. The rise and the fall are simple harmonic. The ticket is four equal arcs. The base is 50 mm and the lift is 30 mm.

Follower radius
50.0

Rules

  1. Angle 0 is the start of the rise. Angle grows clockwise.
  2. On a rise of B degrees, the lift at angle A is half the lift times (1 − cos(180° A / B)).
  3. The fall is the same curve in reverse. A dwell holds the radius still.
  4. The four arcs must sum to 360. Any other sum says The circle is 360 degrees.
  5. The ticket is rise 90, high dwell 90, fall 90, low dwell 90, base 50 mm, lift 30 mm.
  6. At 45° the ticket radius is 65 mm. A match says The follower matches the ticket.
