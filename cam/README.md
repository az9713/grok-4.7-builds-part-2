# Cam

A plate cam and a flat follower. The rise and the fall use simple harmonic motion. The ticket is a 90° rise, a 90° high dwell, a 90° fall, and a 90° low dwell. The base radius is 50 mm. The lift is 30 mm.

At 45° the ticket radius is 65 mm. The four arcs must sum to 360°.
