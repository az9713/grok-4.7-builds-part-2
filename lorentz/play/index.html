Rules

  1. The rest length is 1.00 m.
  2. Length is the square root of (1 − β × β) metres. β is the speed as a fraction of c.
  3. The mark is 0.80 m. A hit is within 0.02 m.
  4. 0.60 c is the hit. The line says The length meets the mark.
  5. 0.00 c reads 1.00 m. 0.40 c reads 0.92 m. 0.80 c reads 0.60 m. 0.90 c reads 0.44 m.
  6. Those four miss. The line says The length misses the mark.
