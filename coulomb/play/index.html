Rules

  1. Each charge is 1 µC.
  2. Force is 0.009 / r² newtons. r is the separation in metres.
  3. The mark is 0.100 N. A hit is within 0.010 N.
  4. 0.30 m is the hit. The line says The force meets the mark.
  5. 0.15 m reads 0.400 N. 0.20 m reads 0.225 N. 0.45 m reads 0.044 N. 0.60 m reads 0.025 N.
  6. Those four miss. The line says The force misses the mark.
