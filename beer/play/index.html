Rules

  1. Absorbance is concentration / 5. Concentration is in mM.
  2. The mark is 1.00. A hit is within 0.02.
  3. 5 mM is the hit. The line says The absorbance meets the mark.
  4. 2 mM reads 0.40. 4 mM reads 0.80. 8 mM reads 1.60. 10 mM reads 2.00.
  5. Those four miss. The line says The absorbance misses the mark.
