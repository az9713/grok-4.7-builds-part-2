Rules

  1. Height is 24 / radius millimetres. Radius is in millimetres.
  2. The mark is 6.0 mm. A hit is within 0.2 mm.
  3. 4 mm is the hit. The line says The height meets the mark.
  4. 2 mm reads 12.0 mm. 3 mm reads 8.0 mm. 6 mm reads 4.0 mm. 8 mm reads 3.0 mm.
  5. Those four miss. The line says The height misses the mark.
