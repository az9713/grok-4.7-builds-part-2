Plate 2 · do not run · ten pitches

Grok 4.6 · Grok Build · round 2

Ten pitches that are not the first eight.

Proposal only. Nothing here is specified for build, and nothing should be generated, cloned, or opened in Unity until one of these is accepted. Each pitch still has a coding half a rival agent can attempt. They lose on Imagine, video, identity lock, or a Rhai swarm that actually ships an artifact.

Status

Not armed. This page is the list. Round 1 stays in this folder as-is.

Already used — do not recycle

Service Door (2D infiltration), Service Door Reel (game trailer), Locksmith (art-bible DAG), Phylum Tray (creature morphs), Sodium Night (passenger short), Closed Stack (press kit), Continuity Bench (face/wardrobe skeptics), Gimbal Goods (lamp catalog + cart). Also banned: AI-wrapper SaaS, chat skins, and any brief Codex or Claude could ship end-to-end.

Show
01

Twin Orbit

Two locked heroes in one gravity-puzzle room. They hand a battery across a gap. Both faces match the title stills.

Game

Grok-only

Two independent character locks. Front/side each. Harvested run, jump, catch cycles from image_to_video. Never a fresh gen after the master.

Coding proof

Browser platformer. Two-player hotseat or sequential control. One room, one win trigger, tests for spawn and handoff radius.

30s clip

Pilot jumps, mechanic throws, catch, door opens. Cut to the two canonical stills beside the sprites.

Rival miss

A playable room whose two people are different every frame, or one hero copy-pasted with a recolor.

Not round 1: Service Door was one operative. Phylum was one animal changing shape. This is two people, one physical interaction.

02

Yard Ledger

Isometric railyard. Dispatch four trains so they do not occupy the same switch. The engine is the same engine on every siding.

Game · sim

Grok-only

Isometric object lock: locomotive + four car types. Autotile track/ballast/signal set with a 2×2 seam check. Idle steam harvested from video.

Coding proof

Occupancy graph, switch states, collision tests, deterministic tick. No physics theatre — a ledger that happens to be drawn isometric.

30s clip

Route a consist around a parked freight. Signal flips. Same locomotive, three angles, no new face.

Rival miss

A sim with clipart trains, or isometric tiles that tear at the seams.

Not round 1: not a side-view stealth room, not a terrarium, not a tileset of a brutalist interior.

03

Ash Court

Eighteen-card microgame. One illustrator lock. Card chrome and type are code. The pictures never contain letters.

Game · tabletop

Grok-only

One ancestor illustration. Seventeen image_edit children (pose, prop, weather). Box art from the same lock. No generated text in bitmaps.

Coding proof

Rules engine in the browser, legal-move tests, print PDF of the rulebook via the PDF skill. Cards are data + overlay, not flattened JPEGs of UI.

30s clip

Deal, play a trick, flip the box. Eighteen pictures, one hand.

Rival miss

Eighteen unrelated drawings, or text baked into the art so the engine cannot restyle a card.

Not round 1: tabletop, not action. PDF rulebook is a surface round 1 never touched.

04

Glass Index

A museum collection site for one sculpture. Sixteen photographs: lens, light, plinth, dust. Search and object pages that actually work.

Site

Grok-only

Sculpture canonical. Every later shot is image_edit from it. Same chip, same fracture, same pedestal wear.

Coding proof

Static collection: index, object, exhibition essay, filter by light/angle. Desktop and mobile checked in a browser. No cart.

30s clip

Scroll sixteen plates. The break in the glass does not wander.

Rival miss

Sixteen different sculptures, or a pretty museum template with stock glass.

Not round 1: Closed Stack was a game press kit. Gimbal Goods was a shop. This is a collection, one object, no checkout.

05

Dieline Press

One bottle. Six flavor colorways. A live SVG dieline and a print-ready PDF. The glass form does not change when the label does.

Site · print

Grok-only

Bottle lock, then six label/liquid edits. Pack shot + three-quarter + top. Same refraction, same shoulder.

Coding proof

Interactive dieline (fold marks, bleed, panel map). PDF export. Colorway switcher that restyles type in code, not in the bitmap.

30s clip

Click “bergamot” to “smoke”. Unfold the net. The bottle is the bottle.

Rival miss

Six unrelated bottles, or a landing page with no dieline.

Not round 1: packaging engineering plus PDF. Not a lamp lookbook with a cart.

06

Night Shift OS

A clickable in-world desktop. Twenty-four icons, four states each. Mail, files, and a map that tell a short story. No HUD, the OS is the game.

Interactive · UI kit

Grok-only

Icon family lock (game-ui-icons discipline): default, hover, active, disabled geometrically identical. Wallpaper from one still. No letters in the bitmaps.

Coding proof

Window manager in the browser. Open, drag, focus, close. Three apps with real content. Keyboard to the end of the story.

30s clip

Boot, open mail, open map, icon states tick. Same mark in the dock and the about-box.

Rival miss

Lucide icons on a dark theme, or generated icons whose hover state is a different drawing.

Not round 1: the UI kit is the product. Service Door never shipped a diegetic computer.

07

Kiln Reel

Six-shot process film of one pot: throw, trim, bisque, glaze, fire, finished. Last-frame continuity on the same vessel.

Film

Grok-only

Pot lock. Each shot is image_edit then image_to_video, seeded from the previous last frame. Hands optional; the clay is the character.

Coding proof

Shot list as data. Player with storyboard | footage | step captions. Deterministic concat. Optional voice via reference_to_video.

30s clip

The short itself. The lip of the bowl does not change identity between wet and glazed.

Rival miss

Six different pots, or a slideshow they cannot shoot.

Not round 1: process documentary, not Sodium Night’s passenger, not a game trailer from Service Door locks.

08

Cutaway House

One house. A dollhouse master, five rooms edited from it, click a room to walk in for six seconds. The floorplan is real SVG.

Film · architecture

Grok-only

Exterior lock. Rooms are image_edit children of a cutaway master (same cornice, same stair). Walk-ins from image_to_video seeded on the still.

Coding proof

Clickable plan, deep links per room, player, no dead buttons. Mobile: plan first, film second.

30s clip

Plan → kitchen walk-in → back to plan → stair. Trim paint does not change colour.

Rival miss

Five unrelated interiors, or an architecture site with stock rooms.

Not round 1: architecture, not a character film and not a studio press site.

09

Broadcast Kit

Invented club, Kite FC. Crest and kit locked. Score bug, lower third, replay wipe, end card — a motion-graphics package, not a story.

Motion

Grok-only

Crest lock, home/away kit lock. HyperFrames compositions for the four graphics. No generated lettering in the bitmaps; type is in the composition.

Coding proof

Composition JSON, preview page, score/clock as data. Overlay a silent 10s clip. Render script, not a one-off After Effects file.

30s clip

Goal, lower third, replay wipe, end card. The kite mark is the same mark.

Rival miss

Generic ESPN-ish overlays, or a crest that mutates between bug and end card.

Not round 1: sports motion-graphics kit. Round 1 film was narrative stills-to-video, never broadcast chrome.

10

Swarm Brief

The Grok 4.6 piece. A Rhai swarm researches one frozen question, draws a locked figure language, and ships a rigorous HTML explainer.

Swarm · 4.6

Grok-only

Long-horizon orchestration (4.6). Imagine for a consistent diagram style (same ink, same section cuts). Watchable fan-out. Not a chat log.

Coding proof

Rhai script, schema, swarm UI, explainer template, citation list. Figures are files the HTML references, not screenshots of a conversation.

30s clip

Agents light up, a bad figure is rejected, the explainer scrolls with matching cutaways.

Rival miss

A markdown essay with unrelated doodles, or a swarm that cannot generate the figures it cites.

Not round 1: Locksmith built an art bible. Continuity Bench scored faces. This ships a knowledge artifact with locked scientific figures.

Suggested later build order, if accepted: 01 Twin Orbit (two locks, smallest playable), 06 Night Shift OS (UI kit), 07 Kiln Reel (process film), 10 Swarm Brief (4.6), then 02 / 03 / 04 / 05 / 08 / 09. Do not start any of them from this page.
