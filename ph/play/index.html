Rules

  1. Concentration is 10 to the chosen power, in mol/L.
  2. pH is −log10 of that concentration.
  3. The mark is 5. A hit is within 0.5.
  4. 1e-5 is the hit. The line says The pH meets the mark.
  5. 1e-3 reads 3. 1e-4 reads 4. 1e-6 reads 6. 1e-7 reads 7.
  6. Those four miss. The line says The pH misses the mark.
