Rules

  1. The level is 10 × log10(ratio) decibels.
  2. The mark is 30 dB. A hit is within 1 dB.
  3. x1000 is the hit. The line says The level meets the mark.
  4. x10 reads 10 dB. x100 reads 20 dB. x10000 reads 40 dB. x100000 reads 50 dB.
  5. Those four miss. The line says The level misses the mark.
