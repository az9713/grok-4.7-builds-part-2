Chain around the pond.

You start at the peg. The mark is 5 units east and 3 units north. A round pond sits on the east line. Bearings run clockwise from north. You may lay 4 legs.

No legs yet.

The mark is open. The pond blocks a straight east chain.

Rules

  1. North is 0°. East is 90°. South is 180°. West is 270°.
  2. Each leg has a whole-degree bearing and a whole distance from 1 to 8.
  3. The chain holds 4 legs. A fifth leg is refused.
  4. The pond center is 2.5 east of the peg, on the east-west line. The radius is 0.9.
  5. A leg that meets the pond is kept, and the chain cannot win while that leg remains. The line says The chain meets the pond.
  6. You win when the free end is within 0.45 of the mark and no leg meets the pond. The line says The chain closes on the mark.
  7. One chain that wins: north 3, then east 5.
