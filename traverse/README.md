# Traverse

A four-leg survey chain. Bearings are degrees clockwise from north. The mark is 5 east and 3 north of the peg. A pond of radius 0.9 is centered 2.5 east of the peg.

A leg that meets the pond blocks a win. The free end must finish within 0.45 of the mark. North 3, then east 5, closes the chain.
