# Service Door Reel

Trailer cut from the Service Door identity locks. Six shots, six seconds each. The playable contractor is the poster.

Open `play/index.html` in a browser (or serve this folder). Shot list is data in `play/shots.json`. View modes: split (storyboard | footage), storyboard only, footage only. Timecode is 24 fps.

## Shots

| # | Slug | Storyboard | Footage |
|---|------|------------|---------|
| 1 | AISLE 3 | `shots/hero-archive.jpg` | still hold |
| 2 | HOLD | `shots/hero.jpg` | `play/video/hero-idle.mp4` |
| 3 | WALK | `shots/hero-archive.jpg` | `play/video/hero-walk.mp4` |
| 4 | PATROL | `shots/guard.jpg` | still hold |
| 5 | FREIGHT | `shots/hero-elevator.jpg` | still hold |
| 6 | CUT | `shots/hero.jpg` | still hold |

Idle and walk cycles are harvested video from the same hero lock. Missing tape is skipped: the player holds the still (Ken Burns) and labels it as a still hold.

Keys: Space, ← →, 1/2/3 for view mode.

## Why this is not a coding-only brief

A rival agent can ship a player. It cannot keep the aisle still, the idle, the walk, and the elevator card as the same person without Imagine edit-chains from a frozen lock. That gap is the reel.
