Rules

  1. The radius is 10 cm. The chord in cm is 20 × sin(angle / 2). The angle is in degrees.
  2. The mark is 10.00 cm. A hit is within 0.005 cm.
  3. 60° is the hit. The line says The chord meets the mark.
  4. 0° reads 0.00 cm. 90° reads 14.14 cm. 120° reads 17.32 cm. 180° reads 20.00 cm.
  5. Those four miss. The line says The chord misses the mark.
