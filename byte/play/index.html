Rules

  1. The power is 2 raised to the exponent.
  2. The mark is 64. A hit is within 0.5.
  3. Power 6 is the hit. The line says The power meets the mark.
  4. Power 4 reads 16. Power 5 reads 32. Power 7 reads 128. Power 8 reads 256.
  5. Those four miss. The line says The power misses the mark.
