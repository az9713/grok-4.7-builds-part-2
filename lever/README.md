# Lever

The left side is 4 kg at 30 cm from the fulcrum. The right side sits at 24 cm. You choose the right-hand mass.

Moment is 4 × 30 − mass × 24, in kg·cm. Level is moment 0, within 0.5 kg·cm. Only 5 kg hits.

A positive moment lowers the left side. The tilt follows the moment.
