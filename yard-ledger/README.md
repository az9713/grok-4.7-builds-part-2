# Yard Ledger

Isometric railyard. Click a switch to flip it. Trains tick on a ledger — two consists collide if they share a cell.

SW0 thrown sends Ash 4 onto the siding. SW1 thrown sends Brick 12 off the main before column 3, then along the siding onto the east spur (columns 5 to 7). A route that stays on the main until column 5 still meets Ash 4 at M3. Both switches normal keeps both consists on the main line.

Locks: `locomotive.jpg`, `boxcar.jpg`, `tanker.jpg`, `track.jpg` from one Imagine isometric camera.

Open `play/index.html`.
