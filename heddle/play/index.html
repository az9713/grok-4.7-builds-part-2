Heddle · four shafts

Weave the twill ticket.

Eight warp ends are threaded 1, 2, 3, 4, and then again. A treadle lifts the shafts tied to it. Where a warp stays down, the weft shows. The ticket is eight picks of 2/2 twill.

Tie-up. Rows are shafts 1 to 4. Columns are treadles 1 to 4.

Throw a pick

Cloth. First pick at the top. Warp is sand. Weft is madder.

Tie the shafts, then throw eight picks.

Rules

  1. Warp end n is on shaft n, counting 1 to 4 and then repeating. Ends 1 and 5 are on shaft 1.
  2. A dark tie-up cell means that shaft rises for that treadle. Click a cell to change it.
  3. A risen warp covers the weft. A warp that stays down shows the weft.
  4. The cloth holds 8 picks. A ninth throw is refused.
  5. Unweave removes the latest pick. The tie-up stays.
  6. The ticket is a 2/2 twill. Treadle 1 lifts shafts 1 and 2. Treadle 2 lifts 2 and 3. Treadle 3 lifts 3 and 4. Treadle 4 lifts 4 and 1.
  7. Throw treadles 1, 2, 3, 4, and then the same four again. The line says The cloth matches the ticket.
  8. Eight picks in any other tie-up or order say The cloth does not match the ticket.
