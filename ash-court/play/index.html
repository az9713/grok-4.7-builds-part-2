Ash Court — rulebook

  1. Eighteen cards, three suits (Moth, Staff, Cloak), ranks 1–6.
  2. Deal nine each. Non-dealer leads.
  3. Follow suit if able. Highest card of the led suit wins.
  4. The bench hand stays face down until that card enters the trick.
  5. First to five tricks wins the sitting.
