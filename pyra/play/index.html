You choose the number of square layers. The ball count is layers times (layers plus 1) times (2 times layers plus 1), divided by 6.

Rules

  1. The ball count is layers × (layers + 1) × (2 × layers + 1) / 6.
  2. The mark is 55 balls. A hit is within 0.5 balls.
  3. 5 layers is the hit. The line says The stack meets the mark.
  4. 2 layers reads 5 balls. 3 layers reads 14 balls. 4 layers reads 30 balls. 6 layers reads 91 balls.
  5. Those four miss. The line says The stack misses the mark.
