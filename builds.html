What each build is

Eighty-two browser builds. Sixty-four of them are games. Each game section lists the rules the page uses. The other pages are films, shops, labs, checks, reports, or instruments.

Game

01 Service Door

Open Service Door

What it is

You walk one night-shift worker through one archive room. A guard walks left and right. A freight lift is on the right. You win when the guard is down and you stand in the lift.

Rules

  1. Press W, A, S, or D to walk. The arrow keys also walk.
  2. The guard keeps walking until you stop the guard.
  3. Stand near the guard. Press Space. The guard falls. Space works when the distance is under 1.15 floor tiles.
  4. Walk into the lift after the guard falls. The line says shift over · R to restart.
  5. Walk into the lift before the guard falls. The lift stays shut. The line says lift locked · put the guard down.
  6. Press R after the win. The room starts again. R does nothing before the win.

07 Continuity Bench

Open Continuity Bench

What it is

This page scores saved pictures against one reference picture. You do not draw the pictures. You read the marks. Click a picture to read the reasons.

How a picture passes

  1. Each picture gets three marks: identity, wardrobe, and lighting.
  2. A mark passes at 0.80 or above, unless the saved run already marks that axis pass or fail.
  3. One failed mark fails that picture. A green mark means that check passed. A red mark means it failed.
  4. When fail-closed is on, one failed picture fails the whole run. The page then shows FAIL.

11 Ash Court

Open Ash Court

What it is

This is a card game for one player against the bench. The deck has 18 cards. You and the bench each receive 9 cards. Your cards are face up. The bench cards stay face down until the bench plays one.

Rules

  1. The suits are Moth, Staff, and Cloak. Each suit has ranks 1 through 6. Rank 6 is the high rank.
  2. You lead the first trick. A trick is two cards. One card comes from you. One card comes from the bench.
  3. Click one of your cards to play it. You play one card in a trick.
  4. When the bench led a suit and you hold that suit, play that suit. When you do not hold that suit, play any card.
  5. The bench plays the led suit when it holds that suit. Otherwise the bench plays the first card in its hand.
  6. The highest rank of the led suit wins the trick. A card from another suit does not win.
  7. The winner of the trick leads the next trick.
  8. The first side to win 5 tricks wins the sitting.
  9. If both hands run out first, the higher trick count wins. An equal count goes to the bench.
  10. The win line is Sitting to you. or Sitting to the bench.
  11. Press Print rulebook to print the short rules on the game page.

19 Apsis

Open Apsis

What it is

You fly one craft around a planet. The craft starts on a low circle. The green line is a higher circle. You do not steer left or right. A burn pushes only along the path.

Rules

  1. Hold Prograde, or hold W, or hold the up arrow. The far point rises. Fuel starts at 0.48. Thrust is 0.12.
  2. Release when the far point meets the green line. The green line is size 1.85.
  3. Coast until Distance meets the far point.
  4. Hold Prograde again. Release when eccentricity is under 0.08 and size is between 1.77 and 1.93.
  5. Hold that coast for 1.2 seconds. The line says Apsis closed. The orbit is round on the outer line.
  6. Retrograde, S, or the down arrow pushes backward. A long retrograde burn drops the craft onto the planet. The line says The craft meets the planet.
  7. A full prograde burn from the inner circle can leave the well. The line says The craft leaves the well.
  8. Press Reset plot, or press R, to start again.

20 Spillway

Open Spillway

What it is

Rain falls on one west tile of a roof. Water walks downhill. The west floor drains into the archive. The east floor drains into the cistern. A stone ridge splits the roof. The top tile of the ridge is open.

Rules

  1. Water moves to a strictly lower neighbor. South wins a tie, then east, then west, then north.
  2. On a flat roof, water creeps east first.
  3. You have 18 moves. Raise spends a move. Lower spends a move. The ridge tiles do not move.
  4. Open the sky. Rain adds 0.3 to the marked tile for 24 steps. The roof then settles for 70 steps.
  5. You win when the cistern holds at least 6.0 and the archive holds under 0.5. The line says The cistern holds the spill.
  6. The archive fails the run at 0.5 or more. The line says Water is in the archive.
  7. A dam that wins: on the row under the rain, raise each of the seven west tiles twice.
  8. Dry the roof keeps your tiles. Restore the yard returns the original heights and the 18 moves.
