Why ships rust at the waterline

Steel in full immersion is starved of oxygen compared with steel in the splash. The wet–dry band at the boot-top becomes an oxygen concentration cell: the aerated splash is cathodic, the waterline is anodic, and iron goes into solution there first.

Mill scale is a cathode against bare steel. Paint holidays at the waterline expose anodes the size of a pin. Cyclic wetting cracks the scale. The swarm’s job was to keep that story to one hull, one plate, and numbers that live in code.
