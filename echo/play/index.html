Rules

  1. Sound moves at 340 m/s.
  2. Distance is 340 × time / 2 metres.
  3. The mark is 34.00 m. A hit is within 0.50 m.
  4. 0.20 s is the hit. The line says The echo meets the cliff.
  5. 0.10 s reads 17.00 m. 0.16 s reads 27.20 m. 0.24 s reads 40.80 m. 0.30 s reads 51.00 m.
  6. Those four miss. The line says The echo misses the cliff.
