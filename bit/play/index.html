Rules

  1. The bit count is log2 of the number of states.
  2. The mark is 3.00 bits. A hit is within 0.005 bits.
  3. 8 states is the hit. The line says The count meets the mark.
  4. 2 states reads 1.00 bits. 4 states reads 2.00 bits. 16 states reads 4.00 bits. 32 states reads 5.00 bits.
  5. Those four miss. The line says The count misses the mark.
