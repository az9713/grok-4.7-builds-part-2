Rules

  1. Each tile has a height. Water moves to a strictly lower neighbor. South wins a tie, then east, then west, then north.
  2. On a flat roof, water creeps east first.
  3. You have 18 moves. Raise spends a move. Lower spends a move. Height stays between 0 and 12.
  4. The ridge tiles do not move.
  5. Press Open the sky. Rain puts 0.3 on the marked west tile for 24 steps, then the roof settles for 70 steps.
  6. You win when the cistern holds at least 6.0 and the archive holds under 0.5. The line says “The cistern holds the spill.”
  7. The archive fails the run when it holds 0.5 or more. The line says “Water is in the archive.”
  8. A known dam: on the row under the rain, raise each of the seven west tiles twice. That is 14 moves. The open ridge at the top lets the water onto the east floor.
  9. Dry the roof clears the water and keeps your tiles. Restore the yard returns the original heights and the 18 moves.
