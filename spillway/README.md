# Spillway

A roof of 16 by 10 tiles. Rain falls on column 4, row 1. Water walks to a lower neighbor. The west floor is the archive. The east floor is the cistern. A stone ridge splits them, with one open tile on the top row.

Raise the seven west tiles on the second row by two. That dam uses 14 of the 18 moves. The cistern then holds the spill and the archive stays under 0.5.
