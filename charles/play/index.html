Rules

  1. The pressure stays fixed.
  2. Volume is 2.00 × temperature / 300 litres. Temperature is in kelvin.
  3. The mark is 2.40 L. A hit is within 0.02 L.
  4. 360 K is the hit. The line says The volume meets the mark.
  5. 273 K reads 1.82 L. 300 K reads 2.00 L. 330 K reads 2.20 L. 400 K reads 2.67 L.
  6. Those four miss. The line says The volume misses the mark.
