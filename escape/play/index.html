Rules

  1. Speed is the square root of 8 × height. Height is in metres. Speed is in m/s.
  2. The mark is 12.00 m/s. A hit is within 0.05 m/s.
  3. 18 m is the hit. The line says The speed meets the mark.
  4. 2 m reads 4.00 m/s. 8 m reads 8.00 m/s. 32 m reads 16.00 m/s. 50 m reads 20.00 m/s.
  5. Those four miss. The line says The speed misses the mark.
