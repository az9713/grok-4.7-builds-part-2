Rules

  1. Critical load is 1000 / (length × length) newtons. Length is in centimetres.
  2. The mark is 40.0 N. A hit is within 0.2 N.
  3. 5 cm is the hit. The line says The load meets the mark.
  4. 2 cm reads 250.0 N. 4 cm reads 62.5 N. 8 cm reads 15.6 N. 10 cm reads 10.0 N.
  5. Those four miss. The line says The load misses the mark.
