Grow the rod to the stop.

The cold length is 1000.00 mm. Each degree Celsius adds 0.02 mm. The stop stands at 1002.00 mm.

Rules

  1. Length is 1000.00 + 0.02 × temperature mm.
  2. Temperature is a whole number from 0 to 200.
  3. The stop is 1002.00 mm. A hit is within 0.009 mm.
  4. 100°C is the hit. The line says The rod meets the stop.
  5. 99°C reads 1001.98 mm. 101°C reads 1002.02 mm. Both miss.
  6. Any other temperature says The rod misses the stop.
