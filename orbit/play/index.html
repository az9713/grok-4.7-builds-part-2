Rules

  1. Speed is 20 / square root of radius. Radius is in metres. Speed is in m/s.
  2. The mark is 4.00 m/s. A hit is within 0.05 m/s.
  3. 25 m is the hit. The line says The speed meets the mark.
  4. 4 m reads 10.00 m/s. 16 m reads 5.00 m/s. 64 m reads 2.50 m/s. 100 m reads 2.00 m/s.
  5. Those four miss. The line says The speed misses the mark.
