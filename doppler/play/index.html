Rules

  1. The listener does not move. The source moves toward the listener.
  2. Heard pitch is 500 × 340 / (340 − speed). Speed is a whole number from 0 to 80.
  3. The crest gap ahead is (340 − speed) / 500 metres. The crest gap behind is (340 + speed) / 500 metres.
  4. The mark is 531.25 Hz. A hit is within 0.5 Hz.
  5. 20 m/s is the hit. The line says The pitch meets the mark.
  6. Any other speed says The pitch misses the mark.
