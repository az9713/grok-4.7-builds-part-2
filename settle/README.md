# Settle

A 10 V step into one resistor and one capacitor. The mark is 6.32 V at 1 second. That voltage is 10 times (1 − e⁻¹), so the time constant is 1 second.

10 kΩ and 100 µF give that time constant. No other pair in the bin lands inside 0.05 V of the mark.
