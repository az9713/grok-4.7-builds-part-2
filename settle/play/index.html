Rules

  1. Voltage is 10 times (1 − e to the power −t / RC). t is in seconds. R is in ohms. C is in farads.
  2. The bin is 1 kΩ, 2.2 kΩ, 4.7 kΩ, 10 kΩ, and 100 µF, 220 µF, 470 µF.
  3. The mark is 6.32 V at 1 second. A hit is within 0.05 V.
  4. 10 kΩ and 100 µF win. The line says The node is at the mark.
  5. Any other pair says The node misses the mark.
