Rules

  1. Speed is 2 × the square root of the depth. Depth is in metres. Speed is in m/s.
  2. The mark is 6.00 m/s. A hit is within 0.05 m/s.
  3. 9 m is the hit. The line says The speed meets the mark.
  4. 1 m reads 2.00 m/s. 4 m reads 4.00 m/s. 16 m reads 8.00 m/s. 25 m reads 10.00 m/s.
  5. Those four miss. The line says The speed misses the mark.
