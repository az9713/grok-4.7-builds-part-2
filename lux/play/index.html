Rules

  1. Illuminance is 100 / distance² lux. Distance is in metres.
  2. The mark is 25.00 lx. A hit is within 0.50 lx.
  3. 2.0 m is the hit. The line says The illuminance meets the mark.
  4. 1.0 m reads 100.00 lx. 2.5 m reads 16.00 lx. 4.0 m reads 6.25 lx. 5.0 m reads 4.00 lx.
  5. Those four miss. The line says The illuminance misses the mark.
