# Snell

A ray leaves liquid into air at one mark on the surface. Exit angle follows Snell's law: sin of the air angle equals the liquid index times sin of the incidence angle. Past the critical angle the ray reflects.

The bell is at x 0.42 and height 0.85. A hit is within 0.03 in x. Index 1.33 at 19° meets the bell.
