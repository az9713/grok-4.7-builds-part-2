Aim the ray. The bell sits 0.42 tank-units to the right of the mark, 0.85 above the surface.

Rules

  1. Air has index 1. The liquid index starts at 1.33 and runs from 1.10 to 1.80.
  2. The ray meets the surface at one fixed mark. Incidence is the angle from the normal, the upright line.
  3. In the air, sin of the exit angle equals the liquid index times sin of the incidence angle.
  4. When that product is above 1, the ray reflects. The line says The ray reflects. The angle is past the critical angle.
  5. The bell is at x 0.42 and height 0.85. You win when the exit ray passes within 0.03 of that x. The line says The ray meets the bell.
  6. One pair that wins: index 1.33 and incidence 19°.
