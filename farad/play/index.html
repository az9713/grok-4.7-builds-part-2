Rules

  1. The capacitance is 2.50 µF.
  2. Charge is (5 × voltage) / 2 microcoulombs.
  3. The mark is 10.0 µC. A hit is within 0.2 µC.
  4. 4 V is the hit. The line says The charge meets the mark.
  5. 2 V reads 5.0 µC. 6 V reads 15.0 µC. 8 V reads 20.0 µC. 10 V reads 25.0 µC.
  6. Those four miss. The line says The charge misses the mark.
