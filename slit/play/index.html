Rules

  1. The wavelength is 500 nm. The screen is 2.00 m from the slit.
  2. The first minimum is 1 / width millimetres, with the width in millimetres.
  3. That position is 2.00 m × 500 nm / width.
  4. The mark is 2.00 mm. A hit is within 0.05 mm.
  5. 0.50 mm is the hit. The line says The minimum meets the mark.
  6. 0.25 mm reads 4.00 mm. 0.40 mm reads 2.50 mm. 0.80 mm reads 1.25 mm. 1.00 mm reads 1.00 mm.
  7. Those four miss. The line says The minimum misses the mark.
