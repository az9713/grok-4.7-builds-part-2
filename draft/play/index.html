Rules

  1. Fresh water density is 1.00 g/cm³.
  2. While hull density is below 1, the submerged fraction equals that density.
  3. Density 1.20 sinks the hull. The line says The hull sinks.
  4. The chalk mark is 0.60 of the hull height. A hit is within 0.02.
  5. 0.60 is the hit. The line says The waterline meets the mark.
  6. 0.40, 0.50, and 0.75 miss. The line says The waterline misses the mark.
