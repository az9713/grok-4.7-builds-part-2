What the dial uses

  1. Declination is 23.44° times sin of 360° times (284 + day) / 365. That is Cooper's formula.
  2. Hour angle is 15° times (hour − 12). Noon is 0°.
  3. sin of altitude is sin(latitude) sin(declination) + cos(latitude) cos(declination) cos(hour angle).
  4. Azimuth is atan2 of sin(hour angle), and cos(hour angle) sin(latitude) − tan(declination) cos(latitude). Positive azimuth is west of south. Negative azimuth is east of south.
  5. Shadow length is 1 / tan(altitude), in gnomon heights. The gnomon height is 1.
  6. At latitude 51.5°, day 81, hour 12, declination is 0° and altitude is 38.5°. That noon altitude is 90° minus the latitude.
  7. Altitude at or below 0° means the sun is down. The line says The sun is below the horizon. This dial has no score.
