Rules

  1. The room volume is fixed.
  2. Reverberation time is 48 / area seconds. Area is in square metres.
  3. The mark is 1.60 s. A hit is within 0.02 s.
  4. 30 m² is the hit. The line says The time meets the mark.
  5. 16 m² reads 3.00 s. 24 m² reads 2.00 s. 40 m² reads 1.20 s. 48 m² reads 1.00 s.
  6. Those four miss. The line says The time misses the mark.
