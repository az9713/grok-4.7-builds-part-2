Rules

  1. The volume stays fixed. The start is 100.0 kPa at 300 K.
  2. Pressure is (100 × temperature) / 300 kilopascals. Temperature is in kelvin.
  3. The mark is 120.0 kPa. A hit is within 0.2 kPa.
  4. 360 K is the hit. The line says The pressure meets the mark.
  5. 300 K reads 100.0 kPa. 330 K reads 110.0 kPa. 390 K reads 130.0 kPa. 450 K reads 150.0 kPa.
  6. Those four miss. The line says The pressure misses the mark.
